THE VISION

The Art of Signals

As an Electronic Engineer with a profound background in fine arts, I don't just see pixels; I see signals, mathematics, and raw aesthetic potential. My research in Computer Vision and 3D Gaussian Splatting allows me to digitize the physical world with unprecedented photorealism.

This is not just 3D modeling. This is the exact preservation of light, texture, and emotion.

THE TECHNOLOGY

Capturing Space

By combining the rigorous mathematical principles of Structure from Motion (SfM) with cutting-edge 3D Gaussian Splatting, I extract the physical world into the digital realm with uncompromised fidelity. Whether it is preserving the grand scale of luxury architectural spaces, or capturing the intricate mechanical elegance and bespoke material finishes of high-end automotive design, I transform static observations into fully navigable, real-time digital twins.

This process goes beyond conventional 3D modeling. It is the exact preservation of spatial reality?”where every specular reflection, organic curve, and subtle interplay of light is meticulously reconstructed and rendered instantaneously in the web browser.
